A little out of place
A little too alive

Not soft
Not hard

Not flame

More like a strange face

This is the shape it chose

Not easy to explain

A little out of line
But impossible to leave

Uneven and alive

Still becoming
Still becoming

From somewhere unclear

No anger
No blade

Refusing to fade

Like a signal from far
Something ancient
Wearing something new

By losing their frame

Did not disappear

And stayed moving here
