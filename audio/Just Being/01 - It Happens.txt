It happens
Before the thought
Before the shape
Before the name

A look
That doesn’t ask
A pause
That holds

No reason
No direction
Just a moment
Opening

It happens
In the quiet
Between
What was
And what stays

Breath stops
Then moves again
Something shifts
Without a sound

No plan
No promise
No need
To follow

It happens
And nothing changes
Except
Everything feels
A little wider
